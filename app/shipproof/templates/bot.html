The Trustfield scanner

Last updated: {{YEAR}}

Trustfield performs passive, read-only checks of public pages. It identifies itself with a User-Agent containing Trustfield and a link to this page.

What it does

  • Requests public URLs (HTML, JavaScript, headers, robots.txt, security.txt, sitemap and a small set of common exposed-file paths).
  • Resolves DNS records for email and TLS trust checks.
  • Never attempts login, exploitation, brute force, data extraction or write operations.

What it does not do

  • No scanning of pages behind authentication.
  • No third-party scanning without the requester confirming ownership or permission.
