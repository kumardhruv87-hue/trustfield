Where Trustfield is the wrong tool

  • You need an exploitation-level pentest or a retest certificate.
  • Your app is behind a login and you need authenticated testing.
  • You need mobile apps, browser extensions or Shopify admin apps reviewed.
  • You need a SOC 2 / ISO style compliance report.

Where it is the right tool

  • You shipped with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor, Replit or by hand and want to know what a stranger can reach.
  • You want the exact fix and a prompt you can paste back into your builder.
  • You want a cheap check after every deploy, with Watch for daily re-checks.
