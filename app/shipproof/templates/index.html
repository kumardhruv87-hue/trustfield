Questions people actually ask

Do I need to connect GitHub?

No. The URL scan works on its own. A public repo adds migration, route-handler and dependency checks — but it is optional, and we never ask for write access.

What exactly is free and what costs ₹{{PRICE_INR}}?

Free: your score and every issue title. Paid, once: evidence for each finding, the exact fix, a copy-paste prompt for your AI builder and the PDF report.

What if a finding is wrong, or the report never arrives?

If we report something factually wrong and you show us why, we refund that report. If the payment succeeds but the report link does not open, we re-run it or refund the full amount within 7 days.

Is this a penetration test?

No, and we will not pretend it is. We passively check what your app serves to the public internet, plus a guided prompt-injection test you run yourself. A full pentest is a different, much more expensive engagement.

Will you scan apps that are not mine?

No. You confirm that you own the app or have permission. We rate-limit scans and we never run exploitation attempts.

What do you store?

The report: findings, evidence snippets and the target URL. We do not keep your source code, and we do not run or store your database. Reports are deleted from our servers 30 days after the scan.

I am not technical. Will I understand it?

That is the design goal. Every finding says what it is, why it matters and what to change — plus a prompt you can paste into the AI that built your app.
