Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 Oct 2026. Plain-language version first — please read the whole page.

What we collect

  • The URL or public repository you submit, so we can run the scan and show you the report.
  • The scan result: findings, short evidence snippets (for example a file path and line number, or a redacted key fragment), and the time of the scan.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to give it.
  • Basic technical data needed to serve a web page: IP address, browser type, and a timestamp. We use it for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect

  • We do not keep your source code. Repository scans are read in memory and discarded.
  • We do not connect to your database, and we do not run exploitation attempts against your app.
  • We do not train models on anything you submit.

Why we process it

To provide the scan and report you asked for, to send you the report link if you gave an email, to prevent abuse of the service, and to keep a minimal record so a report link keeps working.

How long we keep it

Reports and evidence are deleted from our servers 30 days after the scan. Anonymous aggregate statistics (no target, no email) may be kept to publish issue trends. Email addresses are kept until you ask us to delete them. Rate-limiting data is kept for a few hours. Our subprocessors are listed at /subprocessors.

Optional client handover sharing

After a paid re-check, you may choose to publish a limited summary. Nothing is published automatically. The summary contains scores, aggregate comparison counts, scope and dates. It excludes finding titles, evidence snippets, email addresses and private report links. Your app hostname or public repository name is included only if you explicitly choose it.

Anyone holding the summary link can view, download or save it. You can disable the live link from your private re-check report. The link expires when the original report's 30-day window ends; expired and disabled summaries are removed during routine cleanup. Saved copies cannot be recalled. Keep the private report link secret because it includes sharing controls.

Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the product — hosting/CDN, payment processing and transactional email. We do not sell data. We may disclose information if legally required.

Cookies

We use Ahrefs cookieless analytics and anonymous aggregate counts of visits, completed scans, checkouts and purchases to understand whether the product is useful. Campaign sources such as Google or LinkedIn are stored as broad labels; these counters do not contain email addresses, app URLs or personal advertising profiles. Your browser's session storage remembers the broad campaign source for this tab. We do not use advertising cookies. Admin login uses a secure session cookie.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and, where applicable, the GDPR, you also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Children

The service is for businesses and developers and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and, where we have your email, we will tell you.
